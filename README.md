##My first repository
**Stranger Things** is a thrilling science-fiction horror drama series created by the Duffer Brothers for Netflix. Set in the 1980s in the fictional town of Hawkins, Indiana, it follows a group of young kids who uncover supernatural mysteries and secret government experiments.

### Main Characters
* **Eleven** - A young girl with psychokinetic and telepathic abilities.
* **Mike Wheeler** - The loyal and determined leader of the group.
* **Dustin Henderson** - The clever and humorous science enthusiast.
* **Lucas Sinclair** - The tactical and pragmatic member of the team.
